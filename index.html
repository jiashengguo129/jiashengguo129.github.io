About

I am a second-year undergraduate at UC Santa Barbara, where I work in the Qin Lab with Prof. Yao Qin. My research is on LLM agents: how the harness around a model shapes what it can do, and how to improve agents with RL post-training.

Before this I spent a year on diffusion-based image editing, which led to ExpressEdit, a Photoshop plugin for editing stylized facial expressions, presented at a CVPR 2026 workshop.

Research

LLM agents and world models. Since July 2026 I have been working on agents that act over long horizons and adapt to the person they are helping, trained and evaluated in simulation. I am also interested in using mechanistic simulators as world models that an LLM can reason over.

Diffusion-based image editing. From September 2025 to August 2026 I worked on fast and controllable image editing with diffusion models, including multi-GPU parallel inference and biases that build up over repeated edits.
